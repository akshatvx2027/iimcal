Three apps, no app store

All of them open straight in your browser and can be added to your home screen. Bookmark this page and you'll never need to hunt for the links again.

📚

AcadHub

Everything academic, in one place

Your timetable, course outlines, readings and batch announcements — and a nudge the night before with what's on tomorrow, so you know what to have read.

  • Live timetable with rooms, faculty and your own elective picks
  • Session-by-session readings and cases, straight from the course outline
  • Grading breakdown for every subject
  • Pinned announcements for workshops, talks and deadlines
  • One reminder at 8 PM for the next day — or none, your choice

🧺

Laundry Room

Check before you carry the bucket down

Live status of the MDC machines. Check in when you start, see what's free, and join the queue instead of standing next to a running washer.

  • Four washers and two dryers, with who's using what right now
  • Check in when you load — everyone else can see the machine is taken
  • Waitlist when it's all full, and a ping when your turn comes
  • Washers auto-clear after 2 hours, dryers after 1

IIMC OneStop

Food, laundry, xerox & help — one tap away

The campus directory you actually needed in Week 1: what's open, where it is, and the number to call when something goes wrong.

  • Cafeterias and mess points, with menus and hours
  • Open-or-closed status at a glance, right now
  • Xerox and printing, laundry and other campus services
  • Lost & found, leave intimation, medical and emergency contacts
Open OneStop

Add them to your home screen

iPhone: open in Safari → Share ⬆︎ → Add to Home Screen.

Android: open in Chrome → ⋮ → Install app.


They then behave like any other app — full screen, their own icon, and in AcadHub's case, able to send you reminders.
