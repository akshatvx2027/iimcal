Calendar Integration

Sync your AcadHub timetable with Google Calendar and add classes to your phone's calendar app in two ways.

Method 1

Add your courses to Google Calendar

Get a shareable link to all your courses. Paste it into Google Calendar and everything syncs automatically when class times change.

🗓️ Your personal course link

This link contains only the courses you're taking. It updates itself when a class moves or is cancelled, so your Google Calendar always has the latest schedule. Treat it like a password — anyone with this link can see your timetable.

  1. Open AcadHub on your phone
    Go to the Calendar tab (🗓️ at the bottom).
  2. Scroll down to My courses section
    Under "Add these classes to my phone's calendar" you'll see a box with a link.
  3. Tap Copy link
    The link is now in your clipboard. It looks like: https://iimcal-acadhub.netlify.app/cal/...
  4. Keep this link safe
    Anyone with this link can see your timetable. Don't share it publicly, but you can paste it into your personal calendar apps.
ℹ️
Personal to you. This link is unique — it has only YOUR courses, not the whole batch. If you take electives nobody else does, they won't show up on anyone's calendar but yours.
  1. Open Google Calendar on your computer
    Go to calendar.google.com
  2. On the left sidebar, find Other calendars
    Click the + button next to it.
  3. Choose Subscribe to calendar
    A text field appears asking for a URL.
  4. Paste your AcadHub course link
    The link you copied earlier from the Calendar tab.
  5. Click Subscribe
    Google Calendar imports all your courses.
  6. You'll see your schedule in Google Calendar
    It appears on your phone's Google Calendar app automatically. Changes sync within an hour.
✓
Auto-synced. Whenever a class is rescheduled or moved to a different room, your Google Calendar updates automatically. You don't have to do anything.

Method 2

Link your Google Calendar to AcadHub

Connect your personal Google Calendar to AcadHub using a secret code. Then AcadHub can see when you're busy with other commitments — interviews, projects, travel — and warn you about schedule conflicts.

  1. Open AcadHub on your phone
    Go to the Calendar tab (🗓️ at the bottom).
  2. Scroll down to Link your Google Calendar
    You'll see a button that says "Generate secret" or similar.
  3. Tap the button to generate your secret code
    A unique code appears. It looks like: gcal-secret-abc123xyz789...
  4. Copy this code
    Long-press and select "Copy". You'll paste it into Google Calendar next.
  5. Save this secret somewhere safe
    You only need it once. If you lose it, you can generate a new one anytime from this same screen.
🔒
One-way access. This secret only lets AcadHub read your calendar. It cannot add events, delete things, or edit anything. Your Google Calendar stays completely under your control.
  1. Go to Google Calendar Settings
    On calendar.google.com, click the gear icon (⚙️) and choose Settings.
  2. On the left, click Integrate calendar
    It's in the "Settings for my calendars" section.
  3. Find the Secret address in iCal format box
    This is where you'll paste the code from AcadHub.
  4. Paste your AcadHub secret code
    The code you copied from AcadHub goes into this field.
  5. Save the changes
    Google Calendar now has permission to read your calendar.
  6. Done — AcadHub can now see your busy times
    The next time you check AcadHub's Calendar tab, it will show conflicts and warn you about double-bookings.
✓
What this does: AcadHub now knows when you have interviews, project work, or personal plans booked. It can alert you if a class conflicts with something you've already committed to.
⚠️

Conflict warnings

If a club or group asks you to a meeting at 3 PM and you have an interview booked then, AcadHub will flag it in red so you know there's a clash.

Questions

Common issues & answers

The Google Calendar link didn't work

Make sure you're in the right place: Google Calendar Settings → left sidebar → "Integrate calendar" → paste into the field that says "Secret address in iCal format". If it still doesn't work, generate a new secret code from AcadHub and try again.

I lost my course link — how do I get it again?

Go back to AcadHub → Calendar tab → "My courses" → and tap "Copy link" again. It's the same link, so if you already added it to Google Calendar, you don't need to do anything — it's still syncing.

I want to revoke access — how do I do that?

Go back to AcadHub's Calendar tab and tap "Revoke secret". This disables the connection immediately. You can always generate a new secret later if you change your mind.

Can I undo the Google Calendar link?

Yes. In Google Calendar Settings → "Integrate calendar" → delete or clear the secret code field. Your calendar link (Method 1) stays active separately — they're two independent connections.

Who can see my timetable?

Anyone with your course link (Method 1) can see it. The secret code (Method 2) is read-only and only works in Google Calendar. Never share either one publicly — treat them like passwords.
